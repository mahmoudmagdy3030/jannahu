Jannah Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Jannah is a personal worship companion. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or payment processing.

Information stored on your device

Worship history, custom worship entries, settings, notification choices, Quran bookmarks, reciter preferences, and cached Quran data are stored locally on your device. Jannah does not upload this information to a Jannah account or synchronization server. If you export a backup or share a report, you choose where that information is sent.

Location

With permission, Jannah uses precise location to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction, and nearby Miqat information. Coordinates may be sent to BigDataCloud to resolve a city and country name. Manual city searches may be sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. The selected location is stored locally so the app can continue calculating relevant results.

Microphone, speech recognition, and motion

Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you start the optional voice Tasbeeh counter. Speech may be processed by the operating system or browser provider under its privacy terms. Jannah does not store voice recordings. Motion and orientation data are used in real time to point the Qibla compass and are not retained by Jannah.

External content and data services

When you request online content, the relevant provider receives the normal information required for an internet request, such as an IP address and the requested resource. Providers include Quran and audio hosts, YouTube, Google Fonts, Unsplash, gold and exchange-rate services, reverse-geocoding services, and GitHub on supported non-iOS builds. Zakat and inheritance amounts entered in the calculators are processed locally and are not sent to gold or exchange-rate providers.

Provider details are listed in the Third-Party Services and Content Sources. Those providers apply their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Notifications

If enabled, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can change notification permission in system settings and reminder choices inside Jannah.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the app or clear its storage through operating-system controls. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data according to the operating system's behavior.

Children

Jannah is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. It has no account or social feature.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact displayed on Jannah's App Store listing.

ملخص سياسة الخصوصية

لا يتطلب تطبيق جنة إنشاء حساب، ولا يستخدم الإعلانات أو التحليلات أو معالجة المدفوعات. تُحفظ سجلات العبادة والإعدادات والعلامات محلياً على الجهاز. يُستخدم الموقع -بعد الإذن- لحساب المواقيت والقبلة والميقات، وقد تُرسل الإحداثيات إلى خدمة تحديد اسم المدينة. لا يحتفظ التطبيق بالتسجيلات الصوتية. يمكنك حذف البيانات المحلية بإزالة التطبيق أو مسح بياناته من إعدادات نظام التشغيل.
